# Starting Your First Job

Your first few months are about learning how the organisation works and showing that you can be relied on. Arrive on time, meet deadlines, and let your supervisor know early if something will be late.

Ask questions, but try first. Look for the answer in documents or earlier work, then ask with what you already found: "I checked the last report but could not find where the figures come from. Could you show me?"

Write down what you learn: names, processes, policies and the feedback you receive. Keep a simple record of your achievements, as it helps in performance reviews and when updating your CV.

Agree with your supervisor what success looks like after one month and after three months. Ask for feedback regularly rather than waiting for a formal review.

Build good relationships with colleagues at every level. Offer help when you can, keep confidential information private, and keep your communication professional on email and messaging platforms.

Look after yourself too. Plan your finances around your net pay, keep learning in your field, and talk to someone you trust if the workload or environment becomes difficult.
