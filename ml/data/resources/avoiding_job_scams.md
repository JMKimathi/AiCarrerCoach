# Avoiding Job and Internship Scams

Genuine employers do not ask you to pay to apply, to be interviewed, to get a medical check, or to be "processed" for a job. If anyone asks for money at any stage, treat it as a scam.

Be careful with offers that look too good: very high pay for little experience, a job offer without an interview, or pressure to decide within hours. Check that the organisation exists. Look for an official website, a physical address and a company email address rather than a free email account.

For government jobs and internships, apply only through the official portals, such as the Public Service Commission jobs portal or the Teachers Service Commission portal, and check that the advert also appears there. Recruiters who promise to "secure" a government post for a fee are not genuine.

Protect your personal information. Do not send copies of your ID, bank details or KRA PIN until you have a written offer from an organisation you have verified.

For jobs abroad, check that the recruitment agency is registered with the National Employment Authority and read reviews from people who travelled with it.

If something feels wrong, ask your university career office or a trusted lecturer before you go further.
